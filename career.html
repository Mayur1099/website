03 — HOW WE HIRE

Four steps, no mystery.

You will always know which step you're on and what happens next. We've been the candidate on the other side of a black-box process — we swore.

STEP 01

Apply

One short form — no cover-letter theatre. Link what you've built; that's the real CV. Acknowledged within 48 hours, always.

STEP 02

Conversation

Thirty minutes with the team lead. It runs both directions — bring your hardest questions about the stack, the pager and the pay.

STEP 03

Take-home

Time-boxed to four hours, your tools, a real slice of our data. An engineer reads every submission line by line — no keyword filters.

STEP 04

Team day & offer

Half a day with the crew — pair on something real, meet the team, see the lab. Offer within 48 hours of the team day.

EVERYONE GETS AN ANSWER — INCLUDING "NO", WITH A REASON. IF THE TAKE-HOME COST YOU FOUR HOURS, THE LEAST WE OWE YOU IS A SENTENCE ABOUT WHAT WE'D HAVE CHANGED.
