P·01 — GATE IQ · GATE AUTOMATION

The gate that runs itself.

Contactless entry for plants, ports and warehouses — one camera pass, one decision, and the register book retires.

WHAT IT IS

Gate IQ reads the vehicle, checks the paper and releases the truck in one motion. Watch the live sim: the truck rolls onto the inductive loop, the ANPR scan sweeps the plate, telemetry fills in row by row, the policy engine clears the gate pass — and the barrier swings open. Median entry: 3.1 seconds, unattended, in any weather — flip on night mode above and watch it keep reading under IR.

HOW IT WORKS — STEPS TRACK THE LIVE SIM

  1. 01Camera reads plate, vehicle class and condition in a single pass — day, night or monsoon.
  2. 02Policy check against gate pass, purchase order and e-way bill, on the ERP, on-site.
  3. 03Barrier, slip printer and yard direction fire together — one decision, no queue.
  4. 04Exceptions route to a one-screen desk with photo evidence attached. Nothing is ever re-typed.

P·02 — YARD MIND · YARD MANAGEMENT

Every trailer, accounted for.

Camera-based yard management — a live map with a dwell clock on every slot, and no RFID tags to lose.

WHAT IT IS

Fixed cameras re-identify trailers as they move; the yard becomes a live map where every slot carries a dwell timer, and the slotting engine plans moves against dock appointments and ETAs. The 6 a.m. sweep retires, and “where is T-231?” becomes a search bar. Hover the slots above for live dwell — then inject a shift-change rush and watch the surge, and the recovery.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01Cameras re-identify trailers across the yard as they move — 97.1% across angles.
  2. 02A live map with a dwell clock on every slot replaces the whiteboard, wiped daily.
  3. 03The slot optimiser plans tractor moves against dock appointments and inbound ETAs.
  4. 04Exceptions — a lost trailer, an overdue dwell — alert before they cost money.

P·03 — WEIGHSYNC · WEIGHBRIDGE AUTOMATION

Weight you can defend.

Weighbridge automation with a memory — capture, pair, audit and sync, before the truck leaves the platform.

WHAT IT IS

WeighSync captures weight and pairs it to the right vehicle and transaction — gross and tare matched automatically across visits and bridges. An anomaly score flags short-loads, reused tares and double weighments at the platform, and a tamper-evident trail pushes everything to ERP in under two seconds. Arm the fraud test above and watch a bad tare get caught red-handed.

HOW IT WORKS — STEPS TRACK THE LIVE SIM

  1. 01Load-cell capture and vehicle re-ID at the bridge, unattended, day or night.
  2. 02Gross and tare paired automatically — per vehicle, per bridge, across sites.
  3. 03Anomaly score flags short-loads, reused tares and fraud patterns at the platform.
  4. 04Tamper-evident audit trail synced to SAP / Oracle and e-invoice before release.
